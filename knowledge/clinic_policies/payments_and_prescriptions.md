# Payment and Prescription Policies

The clinic manages administrative billing, payment collection, and prescription refill requests according to the policy below. These policies do not replace medical decision-making or veterinary treatment guidance.

## Accepted Payment Methods

The clinic accepts standard payment methods listed in the administrative billing portal or front desk instructions. Payment is expected at the time of service unless a separate account arrangement is approved in advance.

## Payment Plans and Limits

Payment plans are limited to pre-approved arrangements managed through the front desk or billing office. The clinic may decline a payment plan if the request falls outside the current administrative policy or account requirements.

## Prescription Refill Requests

Prescription refill requests must be submitted within the established refill window and may require a sufficient review period. The clinic may delay a refill request if the patient record is not current or if a required veterinary relationship review is incomplete.

## Veterinarian-Client-Patient Relationship

The clinic requires an active veterinarian-client-patient relationship before certain prescription or treatment-related administrative actions may proceed. This requirement is part of the clinic's administrative policy and is not a substitute for veterinary clinical judgment.
