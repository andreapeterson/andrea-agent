# Appointment Policies

This clinic organizes appointments for routine care, follow-up visits, and after-hours administrative requests. The clinic does not provide medical advice through this policy summary.

## Arrival and Check-In

Clients should arrive a few minutes before their scheduled appointment time so the front desk can confirm contact information and pet details. Late arrivals may require a shortened visit or rescheduling based on the clinic's availability.

## Cancellation and Rescheduling

Appointments may be cancelled or rescheduled in advance through the normal booking flow. Requests received with sufficient notice are generally accommodated when staff capacity allows.

## Missed Appointments

A missed appointment may be marked as a no-show if the client does not arrive or does not provide timely notice. Repeated missed appointments may require a new scheduling review before additional visits are booked.
