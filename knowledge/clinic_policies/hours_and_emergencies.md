# Clinic Hours and After-Hours Emergencies

Our clinic is open for regular daytime appointments during the posted business hours. Clients may use the contact portal to check current open hours before booking.

## Regular Hours

The clinic maintains regular weekday office hours for routine appointments and administrative questions. After-hours calls are routed to the secure intake flow and do not create medical diagnoses.

## After-Hours Urgent Concerns

When PawLine identifies an urgent concern, the case moves to human escalation through the standard handoff process. This is not a substitute for emergency veterinary treatment and does not provide medical advice.

## Emergency Contact Steps

Clients should follow the clinic's intake and handoff instructions for urgent concerns. Staff may direct clients to emergency veterinary services if the situation requires immediate care outside the clinic's operating hours.
